The repository is organized as a multi-service monorepo where each child module owns one deployable surface and they are bound together at the root:
- `backend/` (FastAPI + Celery + PostgreSQL/pgvector) implements the transaction screening pipeline — idempotency check, hard-rule engine, behavioral monitor, classifier, risk scorer, gating logic, case management, SAR/STR filing, and pgvector RAG retrieval.
- `platform-app/` is the Next.js 14 App Router dashboard consuming `/api/v1/*` over HTTP, sharing the same JWT/API-key auth contract with the backend.
- `website_marketing/` (rooted at `website/`) is a separate static nginx site for landing/pricing/about pages, served independently from the compose stack.
- `deployment_ops/` (`docker-compose.yml`, three Dockerfiles, `website/nginx.conf`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `DEPLOYMENT.md`, `DOCKER.md`) is the single source of truth for how those services are built, composed, and shipped to Railway/Vercel or Kubernetes.
Cross-child wiring happens at the repo root: `README.md` declares the shared API contract (`/api/v1/transactions`, idempotency keys, health endpoint), `docker-compose.yml` binds ports 3000 (frontend) and 8091 (backend) and shares env vars like `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL`, and `DEPLOYMENT.md`/`DOCKER.md` document the two deployment paths (local compose vs. production on Railway+Vercel). The website is not part of the compose stack — it is served independently by nginx in its own container. The OpenAPI schema at `/api/docs` is the authoritative contract between frontend and backend; the Wiki and `docs/architecture/evolution/` track the gap between target design and implemented code.
