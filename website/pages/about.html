Building the compliance infrastructure the world needs

We believe financial compliance shouldn't slow innovation. FinShield exists to give every financial institution — from startups to global banks — the same compliance power as the biggest players in the industry.

Our Story

FinShield was founded in 2023 by a team of compliance engineers and ML researchers who watched fintechs struggle with the same problem: legacy compliance systems that were slow, expensive, and couldn't scale.

Traditional AML/CTF solutions were built for a different era — batch-processed, rule-only, and riddled with false positives. We set out to build something fundamentally different: a local-first, AI-powered compliance engine that processes transactions in milliseconds, not hours.

Today, FinShield screens over 2.4 million transactions daily across 247 financial institutions in 34 countries. Our platform combines hard regulatory rules with behavioral ML models to deliver compliance that's both precise and adaptive.

We're backed by leading investors in fintech and regtech, and we're growing fast. But our mission hasn't changed: make world-class compliance accessible to everyone.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we build, from architecture to customer support.

Compliance First

Every feature we ship must meet regulatory standards. We don't move fast and break things — we move fast and stay compliant.

Local-First

Your data stays on your infrastructure. We never touch customer transaction data. Privacy isn't a feature — it's the architecture.

Performance

Compliance shouldn't be slow. Our pipeline processes transactions in under 200ms with sub-50ms P99 for rule-only screening.

Transparency

Every decision our AI makes is explainable. No black boxes. Regulators and auditors can trace every flag back to its source.

Customer Obsessed

Our customers' compliance success is our success. Dedicated support, custom integrations, and a product roadmap shaped by user feedback.

Open Standards

Built on open protocols and standards. REST APIs, OpenTelemetry, standard ML formats. No vendor lock-in, ever.
